The short version

A Thai tonic, an Austrian marketer and one idea about energy.

Red Bull started as Krating Daeng, a tonic sold in Thailand. An Austrian marketing executive, Dietrich Mateschitz, tasted it on a work trip, struck a partnership with its creator and rebuilt it as a slim silver and blue can with its own sparkling recipe. Then the company did something unusual. It put most of its marketing into events, athletes and its own media.

Timeline

From a tonic to a team

1976

Krating Daeng

Chaleo Yoovidhya launches the tonic in Thailand. The name means red gaur, a large wild bull.

1984

A partnership

Mateschitz and Yoovidhya found Red Bull GmbH, each holding 49 percent, and spend three years adapting the product.

1987

First Austrian can

Red Bull goes on sale in Austria. The slogan, “Red Bull gives you wings”, follows soon after.

1997

Into the US

The first American market is California, after launches across Europe.

2005

A Formula 1 team

The company buys the Jaguar F1 team and races as Red Bull Racing. Its first drivers' title comes in 2010.

2012

Stratos

Felix Baumgartner steps off a capsule at the edge of space. Over 8 million people watch live online.

2022

A new chapter

Dietrich Mateschitz dies in October at age 78. The company keeps its sport and media model.

The numbers

Twelve billion cans

Annual unit sales have almost doubled in five years. The biggest single jump came in 2021, when sales rose by nearly two billion cans.
